📜 Sthala Purana & Sacred Lore

Origin of Haridwar Mansarovar Temple

The temple is believed to have been built by King Vikramaditya in the 5th century CE. It is dedicated to Lord Shiva, and is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas. The temple is located on the banks of the Ganges River, and is said to be the place where the Ganges descended from heaven to earth. The temple is also said to be the place where Lord Shiva performed the tandava dance, which created the universe. The temple is a popular pilgrimage site for Hindus, and is visited by millions of devotees every year.

Mention in Hinduism Scripture

The Haridwar Mansarovar Temple is mentioned in the Puranas, which are a collection of ancient Hindu texts. The temple is also mentioned in the Mahabharata, which is one of the two major epics of Hinduism. The temple is said to be one of the most sacred places in Hinduism, and is believed to be a place where one can attain moksha, or liberation from the cycle of rebirth.
