📜 Sthala Purana & Sacred Lore

Origin of Jakhu Temple, Himachal Pradesh

The Jakhu Temple, located in Himachal Pradesh, India, holds great significance in Hinduism. This ancient temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman, a popular deity in Hindu mythology known for his strength, devotion, and loyalty. The temple's origin can be traced back to an intriguing story mentioned in Hindu scriptures.

1. The Legend of Lord Hanuman's Visit to Jakhu Hill According to the Hindu epic Ramayana, Lord Hanuman was sent by Lord Rama to fetch a magical herb from the Himalayas to heal his wounded brother, Lakshmana. While searching for the herb, Lord Hanuman stumbled upon the Jakhu Hill in the region of Himachal Pradesh.

2. Lord Hanuman's Encounter with Demon Kalnemi As Lord Hanuman reached the Jakhu Hill, he encountered a powerful demon named Kalnemi. The demon disguised himself as a sage and tried to deceive Lord Hanuman. However, Lord Hanuman recognized the demon's true identity and engaged in a fierce battle. After a long and arduous fight, Lord Hanuman emerged victorious, defeating the demon.

3. The Creation of Jakhu Temple Following the battle with Kalnemi, Lord Hanuman decided to take rest on the Jakhu Hill. Impressed by Lord Hanuman's valor, the local villagers requested him to stay and bless their land. Pleased with their devotion, Lord Hanuman granted their wish. The villagers then constructed a temple in his honor, which eventually came to be known as the Jakhu Temple.

4. The Sacred Hanuman Statue One of the main attractions of the Jakhu Temple is the presence of a massive statue of Lord Hanuman. This statue stands at a staggering height of 108 feet, making it one of the tallest Hanuman statues in the world. Devotees believe that visiting the Jakhu Temple and seeking the blessings of Lord Hanuman brings good luck, strength, and protection.

5. The Annual Hanuman Jayanti Festival Every year, the Jakhu Temple witnesses a grand celebration during the festival of Hanuman Jayanti. Devotees from all over the country gather at the temple to pay homage to Lord Hanuman. The festival involves various rituals, prayers, and cultural performances, creating a vibrant and joyous atmosphere.

Conclusion The Jakhu Temple in Himachal Pradesh holds immense significance in Hinduism due to its connection with Lord Hanuman. The legend of Lord Hanuman's visit to Jakhu Hill and the subsequent construction of the temple showcases the devotion and faith of the local villagers. The temple continues to be a popular pilgrimage site, attracting devotees who seek Lord Hanuman's blessings and protection.
