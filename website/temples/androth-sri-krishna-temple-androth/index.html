The Androth Sri Krishna Temple is one of the oldest temples in the Lakshadweep Islands. It is believed to have been built in the 11th century by the Chera dynasty. The temple is dedicated to Lord Krishna, and is one of the most important pilgrimage sites in the Lakshadweep Islands.

The Androth Sri Krishna Temple is a beautiful example of Keralan temple architecture. The temple is built in the traditional Keralan style, with a high, pyramidal roof and a large, open courtyard.
