📜 Sthala Purana & Sacred Lore

Shri Mahadev Mandir, Silvassa

Location: Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli History: The temple was built in the 19th century by the Koli community. Architecture: The temple is built in the Gujarati style of architecture. Deity: The main deity of the temple is Lord Shiva. Festivals: The temple is especially popular during the Shivratri festival. Significance: The temple is a popular pilgrimage site for Hindus from all over the country.
