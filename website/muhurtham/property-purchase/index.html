What is Property Purchase Muhurtham?

Purchasing real estate or land is a life-defining milestone. Selecting an auspicious cosmic hour ensures the property brings prosperity, value appreciation, and generational peace.

🕉 Religious & Vedic Significance

Propitiates Bhudevi and Lord Kuvera to bestow enduring abundance upon the buyer.

✨ Why is Muhurtham Selected?

Shields against fraudulent titles, encumbrances, and hidden legal pitfalls.

📜 Classical Selection Principles & Doshas Avoided

Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Magha, Visakha, Anuradha, and Revati are favored. Thursday and Friday are best.

📋 Information Required for Calculation:

Buyer name, property category (plot, flat, land), location, and date range.
