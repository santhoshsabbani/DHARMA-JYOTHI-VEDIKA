What is Annaprashana Muhurtham?

Annaprashana marks the child's transition from milk to solid grain food. Usually performed in even months (6th, 8th) for male infants and odd months (5th, 7th) for female infants.

🕉 Religious & Vedic Significance

Mother Annapurna is invoked to ensure the child never suffers from hunger or digestive ailments.

✨ Why is Muhurtham Selected?

Safeguards the infant's developing digestive constitution and bestows physical vitality.

📜 Classical Selection Principles & Doshas Avoided

Avoid Rikta Tithis and Amavasya. Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashira, Pushya, Hasta, Anuradha, and Revati are favored.

Baby DOB, gender, parents' names, ceremony venue, and date range.
