Step-by-Step 16-Upachara Puja Vidhi

The authentic Vedic worship for Dhanteras (Dhantrayodashi) follows the sacred Shodashopachara Vidhi:

  1. Dhyana & Avahana: Meditate on the divine form of Lord Dhanvantari / Kubera / Lakshmi and invoke presence into the sacred vigraha/kalash.
  2. Asana: Offer a seat of sacred flowers or silk cloth.
  3. Padya & Arghya: Offer pure sanctified water for washing feet and hands.
  4. Achamana: Offer water mixed with cardamom and camphor for sipping.
  5. Snana / Panchamrita Abhishekam: Bathe with pure water, milk, curd, honey, ghee, and sugar.
  6. Vastra & Upavita: Offer sacred red/yellow cloth and Yajnopavita.
  7. Gandha & Chandanam: Anoint with fragrant Malayagiri sandalwood paste and Kumkuma.
  8. Pushpa & Bilva/Tulasi: Offer fresh fragrant flowers and sacred leaves.
  9. Dhupa: Wave fragrant dhoop sticks and guggul.
  10. Deepa: Wave pure cow ghee lamp reciting the Deepa Mantra.
  11. Naivedyam: Offer freshly prepared satvik food, kheer, fruits, and betel leaves.
  12. Tambula: Offer betel leaves, supari, and dakshina.
  13. Nirajana (Maha Mangala Harati): Perform camphor Aarti singing devotional stotras.
  14. Pradakshina & Namaskara: Circumambulate clockwise 3 times and prostrate with devotion.
  15. Kshama Prarthana: Seek forgiveness for any unintentional mistakes during worship.

Fasting (Vrat) Rules & Observance Guidelines

Light Yamadeepam facing South outside the house entrance at dusk.

Essential Samagri & Naivedyam

Required Puja Samagri: Four-wick earthen lamp (Choumukha diya), Sesame oil, Brass utensil or gold/silver coin, Coriander seeds (Dhaniya)

Traditional Naivedyam (Prasadam): Panchamritam, Kheer, Whole coriander seeds mixed with jaggery, Seasonal fruits

Dos & Don'ts for Dhanteras (Dhantrayodashi)

Dos (Auspicious Actions): Buy brass utensils or gold, light Yamadeepdaan facing South, worship Dhanvantari for health.

Don'ts (Practices to Avoid): Do not purchase sharp iron items, glass, or black objects on Dhanteras.
